Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is IPTV and how does it work?
IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers television content through internet networks instead of traditional cable or satellite systems. When you select a channel or video, our servers stream the content directly to your device over your internet connection. This technology allows for greater flexibility, more channels, better picture quality, and the ability to watch on multiple devices anywhere with an internet connection.
What internet speed do I need for smooth streaming?
For standard HD quality, we recommend a minimum of 10 Mbps internet connection. For Full HD and 4K content, 25 Mbps or higher is ideal. Our adaptive streaming technology automatically adjusts quality based on your connection speed to minimize buffering. For the best experience, we suggest using a wired Ethernet connection when possible, though WiFi works well with strong signals.
Can I watch on multiple devices simultaneously?
Yes! Depending on your subscription plan, you can stream on multiple devices at the same time. Our standard plans support simultaneous connections on different devices, perfect for families where everyone wants to watch different content. Simply log in with your credentials on each device, and you're ready to go.
